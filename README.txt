Luxtronik Heat Pump Manager brings your Luxtronik-based heat pump into Homey — with full sensor visibility, smart flow integration, and direct control over heating and hot water. Whether you want to automate hot water heating with solar surplus, adjust operating modes, or get notified when your heat pump changes state, this app gives you the tools to do it.

Compatible with Alpha-Innotec, CTA, Novelan, Roth, Elco, Buderus, Nibe, Wolf Heiztechnik and many other Luxtronik-based systems. The app uses the standardised Luxtronik interface and works across different system configurations and firmware versions.
